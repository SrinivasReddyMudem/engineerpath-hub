Privacy

Last updated:

What EngineerPath.hub collects

Only what's needed to actually help you:

  • Ask EngineerPath form (via Tally): name, email, what you need help with, your experience level, and what you're trying to achieve.
  • Application Tracker and any other tools: stored only in your own browser (localStorage) — never sent to us or anyone else.

Never collected: CV/resume uploads, passports, financial documents, or any other sensitive data the support workflow doesn't strictly require.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your question or request through the Ask EngineerPath form. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for purposes beyond that.

Third-party services

Form submissions are processed by Tally, subject to their own privacy policy. If analytics are added later, they'll be a cookieless, privacy-friendly tool, and this page will be updated first.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time — contact us via the Ask EngineerPath form or .
