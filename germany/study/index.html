🎓 Germany · Study

Study engineering in Germany

Public German universities are low or no tuition even for international students — a real reason this route is popular. Universities, scholarships, applications and housing all have one authoritative source each; we point to those directly rather than restate rules that change.

Course & university search

DAAD International Programmes →

21,000+ internationally-oriented degree programmes, filterable by subject, level, language and tuition — with a built-in bookmark list. The official starting point for "which university, which course."

Scholarships & funding

DAAD Scholarships →

The official German Academic Exchange Service database — scholarship programmes for international students and researchers, filterable by country and level.

Grade conversion

uni-assist →

The real, legally-recognized conversion of your existing grades to the German scale, used by most German public universities. The Job Readiness Checker's grade estimate is a quick preview only — this is the binding one at application time.

Student housing

DSW — accommodation for international students →

The official non-profit body running Germany's student dorms nationwide. This is where the real housing search and application happens.
